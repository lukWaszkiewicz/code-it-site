What I can do for your project

I can deliver a whole change end to end, or take on one area your team doesn't have the time or experience for.

System architecture

Architecture for large CRMs integrated with external systems: solution design across several clouds, scalable data models, sharing and visibility, identity and access, integration patterns, and an architecture you can document and defend in a design review.

Org quality and security audit

A review of code, automation, the data model, sharing, permissions, Security Health Check results and governor-limit risks. The result is a clear report with priorities and effort estimates.

CI/CD and release management

A Git branching strategy, pipelines with Salesforce CLI and delta deployments, automated validation, tests and static code analysis, and releases that are predictable and repeatable.

Data migration

I lead large migrations from legacy databases, other CRMs and other Salesforce orgs, covering ETL, cleanup, field and value mapping, large data volumes, and validation with the business before go-live.

Legacy modernisation

Refactoring legacy Apex into enterprise patterns, moving Workflow Rules and Process Builder to Flow, rebuilding Visualforce and Aura in LWC, and moving legacy systems onto Salesforce.

Development and integrations

Apex, LWC, Flow, REST and SOAP integrations, Platform Events, Experience Cloud portals and Agentforce, all bulk-safe, tested and ready for production.
